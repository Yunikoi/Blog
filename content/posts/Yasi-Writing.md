> 雅思大作文话题高频词组（每类 20 个）。格式：`#### 词组：释义 | 用法` + `>` 搭配 / 例句骨架。

## 教育类

#### broaden one's horizons：拓宽视野

> Education can **broaden students' horizons** beyond their local communities.

#### acquire knowledge and skills：获取知识与技能

> Schools should help pupils **acquire the knowledge and skills** needed in the job market.

#### rote learning：死记硬背

> Over-reliance on **rote learning** may stifle creativity.

#### critical thinking：批判性思维

> Universities ought to cultivate **critical thinking** rather than exam techniques alone.

#### lifelong learning：终身学习

> In a fast-changing economy, **lifelong learning** has become a necessity.

#### equal access to education：教育机会均等

> Governments should ensure **equal access to education** for rural and urban children.

#### tuition fees：学费

> Rising **tuition fees** may deter talented students from low-income families.

#### academic performance：学业表现

> **Academic performance** should not be the sole measure of a school's quality.

#### vocational training：职业培训

> **Vocational training** can address skills shortages in manufacturing and services.

#### literacy and numeracy：读写与算术能力

> Basic **literacy and numeracy** remain foundations of any education system.

#### curriculum reform：课程改革

> **Curriculum reform** is needed to reflect digital and environmental challenges.

#### extracurricular activities：课外活动

> **Extracurricular activities** help develop teamwork and leadership.

#### distance learning：远程学习

> **Distance learning** expanded rapidly during the pandemic.

#### teacher shortage：师资短缺

> A chronic **teacher shortage** undermines education quality in remote areas.

#### drop out of school：辍学

> Many teenagers **drop out of school** due to economic pressure.

#### bridge the gap：缩小差距

> Scholarships can **bridge the gap** between rich and poor students.

#### foster creativity：培养创造力

> Arts education helps **foster creativity** and emotional expression.

#### exam-oriented education：应试教育

> An **exam-oriented education** system may produce stress rather than genuine learning.

#### tertiary education：高等教育

> **Tertiary education** should balance theory with practical experience.

#### instill moral values：灌输道德价值观

> Parents and schools should **instill moral values** in the young.

#### replace human labour：取代人力

#### online shopping：网上购物

#### genetic engineering：基因工程

#### crime rate：犯罪率

> CCTV and policing may lower the **crime rate** in city centres.

#### deter crime：威慑犯罪

> Harsh sentences alone may not **deter crime** effectively.

#### commit a crime：犯罪

> Poverty alone does not explain why people **commit a crime**.

#### juvenile delinquency：青少年犯罪

> **Juvenile delinquency** is linked to family breakdown and peer pressure.

#### capital punishment：死刑

> **Capital punishment** remains controversial on moral and practical grounds.

#### life imprisonment：无期徒刑

> **Life imprisonment** is an alternative to the death penalty.

#### rehabilitation：改造；康复

> Prisons should focus on **rehabilitation** as well as punishment.

#### reoffend：再犯

> Without job training, ex-prisoners are more likely to **reoffend**.

#### law-abiding citizens：守法公民

> Most people are **law-abiding citizens** who respect social rules.

#### strict penalties：严厉惩罚

> **Strict penalties** may reduce certain crimes but not address root causes.

#### surveillance cameras：监控摄像头

> **Surveillance cameras** can prevent theft but raise privacy concerns.

#### cyber fraud：网络诈骗

> **Cyber fraud** has surged with the growth of online banking.

#### organised crime：有组织犯罪

> **Organised crime** networks profit from drugs and human trafficking.

#### drug trafficking：贩毒

> **Drug trafficking** fuels violence in some border regions.

#### prison overcrowding：监狱人满为患；监狱过度拥挤

> **Prison overcrowding** worsens conditions and hinders rehabilitation.

#### address the root causes：解决根本原因

> Policy must **address the root causes** of crime, such as poverty and unemployment.

#### victim of crime：犯罪受害者

> Support services should help every **victim of crime** recover.

#### community policing：社区警务

> **Community policing** builds trust between officers and residents.

#### white-collar crime：白领犯罪

> **White-collar crime** such as embezzlement costs economies billions.

#### break the law：违法

> Ignorance of regulations does not excuse those who **break the law**.
