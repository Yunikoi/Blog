## 作文关键动词（20）

> 用法：每段主体段先选 **1 个动词** → 套 `[政府 / 学校 / 家长 / 企业] + should / can + 动词 + 具体内容`。下列每词含 **核心搭配** + **六大话题例句**。

#### require：需要；要求

> **核心搭配**
>
> - require sb. to do sth. 要求某人做…
> - require effort / patience / dedication / funding 需要努力 / 耐心 / 投入 / 资金
> - be required by law / regulation 法律 / 法规要求
> - meet the requirements 满足要求
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：University degrees **require** students to complete independent research.
> - **科技**：Advanced software **requires** regular updates to remain secure.
> - **环境**：Tackling climate change **requires** global cooperation.
> - **社会/工作**：Many jobs now **require** digital skills.
> - **政府/钱**：Healthcare reform **requires** substantial public funding.
> - **犯罪**：Effective policing **requires** trust between officers and communities.

#### encourage：鼓励；促进

> **核心搭配**
>
> - encourage sb. to do sth. 鼓励某人做…
> - encourage innovation / creativity / participation 鼓励创新 / 创造力 / 参与
> - highly encouraged 强烈建议（正式）
> - encourage economic growth 促进经济增长
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Teachers should **encourage** pupils to ask questions.
> - **科技**：Tax breaks can **encourage** investment in green technology.
> - **环境**：Subsidies may **encourage** firms to cut carbon emissions.
> - **社会/工作**：Flexible policies **encourage** women to return to work.
> - **政府/钱**：Low interest rates **encourage** consumer spending.
> - **犯罪**：Community programmes **encourage** young people to stay away from crime.

#### prevent：阻止；防止

> **核心搭配**
>
> - prevent sb. from doing sth. 阻止某人做…
> - prevent accidents / disease / crime / damage 防止事故 / 疾病 / 犯罪 / 损害
> - take measures to prevent 采取措施预防
> - prevention is better than cure 防胜于治
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Early intervention can **prevent** students from dropping out.
> - **科技**：Firewalls **prevent** hackers from accessing data.
> - **环境**：Protected areas **prevent** deforestation.
> - **社会/工作**：Labour laws **prevent** employers from exploiting workers.
> - **政府/钱**：Financial regulation **prevent** banks from taking excessive risks.
> - **犯罪**：Street lighting may **prevent** petty theft at night.

#### reduce：减少；降低

> **核心搭配**
>
> - reduce the number / level / cost / risk of 减少数量 / 水平 / 成本 / 风险
> - reduce traffic / pollution / waste / inequality 减少拥堵 / 污染 / 浪费 / 不平等
> - significantly / dramatically reduce 显著减少
> - reduce dependence on 减少对… 的依赖
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Smaller classes can **reduce** stress among pupils.
> - **科技**：Automation may **reduce** production costs.
> - **环境**：Public transport helps **reduce** air pollution.
> - **社会/工作**：Remote work can **reduce** commuting time.
> - **政府/钱**：Progressive tax may **reduce** income inequality.
> - **犯罪**：Higher employment **reduces** the crime rate.

#### improve：改善；提高

> **核心搭配**
>
> - improve the quality / standard / efficiency of 提高质量 / 标准 / 效率
> - improve people's lives / health / skills 改善生活 / 健康 / 技能
> - improve performance / productivity 提高表现 / 生产力
> - improve access to 改善对… 的获取
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Teacher training **improves** classroom outcomes.
> - **科技**：5G networks **improve** communication speed.
> - **环境**：Recycling **improves** resource efficiency.
> - **社会/工作**：Childcare support **improves** work-life balance.
> - **政府/钱**：Infrastructure spending **improves** economic competitiveness.
> - **犯罪**：Rehabilitation **improves** prisoners' chances of finding work.

#### affect：影响（可正可负，写作常指负面影响）

> **核心搭配**
>
> - affect sb. / sth. 影响…
> - adversely / negatively affect 不利影响
> - be affected by 受… 影响
> - affect mental health / performance / the environment 影响心理健康 / 表现 / 环境
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Poverty **affects** children's academic performance.
> - **科技**：Social media **affects** teenagers' self-esteem.
> - **环境**：Industrial waste **affects** local water quality.
> - **社会/工作**：Automation **affects** low-skilled employment.
> - **政府/钱**：Inflation **affects** purchasing power.
> - **犯罪**：Unemployment **affects** the likelihood of reoffending.

#### provide：提供；供应

> **核心搭配**
>
> - provide sb. with sth. / provide sth. for sb. 向某人提供…
> - provide access / support / funding / opportunities 提供渠道 / 支持 / 资金 / 机会
> - provide free healthcare / education 提供免费医疗 / 教育
> - provide a solution / alternative 提供解决方案 / 替代方案
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Libraries **provide** free learning resources.
> - **科技**：Apps **provide** instant access to information.
> - **环境**：National parks **provide** habitats for wildlife.
> - **社会/工作**：Unions **provide** legal support for workers.
> - **政府/钱**：The state should **provide** a basic safety net.
> - **犯罪**：Victim support centres **provide** counselling services.

#### achieve：实现；达到（目标、成功）

> **核心搭配**
>
> - achieve a goal / target / balance / success 实现目标 / 平衡 / 成功
> - achieve equality / sustainability 实现平等 / 可持续
> - difficult to achieve 难以实现
> - achieve academic excellence 取得优异学业成绩
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Only through effort can students **achieve** their potential.
> - **科技**：Countries compete to **achieve** technological leadership.
> - **环境**：Net-zero targets are hard to **achieve** without investment.
> - **社会/工作**：Training helps workers **achieve** career goals.
> - **政府/钱**：Fiscal discipline helps **achieve** budget stability.
> - **犯罪**：Restorative justice aims to **achieve** reconciliation.

#### allow：允许；使成为可能

> **核心搭配**
>
> - allow sb. to do sth. 允许某人做…
> - allow for 考虑到；留出余地
> - allow greater flexibility / freedom 允许更大灵活性 / 自由
> - not allow / forbid 不允许
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Online courses **allow** adults to study part-time.
> - **科技**：Cloud storage **allows** data to be shared globally.
> - **环境**：Solar panels **allow** homes to generate clean energy.
> - **社会/工作**：Hybrid work **allows** employees to save commuting time.
> - **政府/钱**：Tax incentives **allow** small firms to reinvest profits.
> - **犯罪**：Bail conditions **allow** suspects to remain in the community.

#### cause：导致（通常接坏结果）

> **核心搭配**
>
> - cause problems / damage / harm / pollution 造成问题 / 损害 / 伤害 / 污染
> - cause concern / debate 引发担忧 / 争论
> - be caused by 由… 引起
> - root cause 根本原因
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Exam pressure can **cause** anxiety among teenagers.
> - **科技**：Data breaches **cause** financial losses.
> - **环境**：Fossil fuels **cause** greenhouse gas emissions.
> - **社会/工作**：Long hours **cause** burnout.
> - **政府/钱**：Speculation **causes** housing bubbles.
> - **犯罪**：Drug addiction **causes** many property crimes.

#### promote：促进；推广

> **核心搭配**
>
> - promote economic growth / equality / health 促进经济增长 / 平等 / 健康
> - promote innovation / cooperation / understanding 推动创新 / 合作 / 理解
> - promote a product / idea 推广产品 / 理念
> - promote social mobility 促进社会流动
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Arts education **promotes** creativity.
> - **科技**：Open-source software **promotes** collaboration.
> - **环境**：Eco-labels **promote** sustainable consumption.
> - **社会/工作**：Mentoring **promotes** career development.
> - **政府/钱**：Free trade agreements **promote** cross-border investment.
> - **犯罪**：Youth clubs **promote** law-abiding behaviour.

#### address：处理；解决；阐述

> **核心搭配**
>
> - address a problem / issue / concern 处理问题 / 议题 / 担忧
> - address the root causes 解决根本原因
> - address inequality / unemployment 应对不平等 / 失业
> - fail to address 未能解决
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Reforms must **address** the teacher shortage.
> - **科技**：Laws should **address** data privacy risks.
> - **环境**：Policies need to **address** water scarcity.
> - **社会/工作**：Governments must **address** the cost-of-living crisis.
> - **政府/钱**：Budgets should **address** regional disparities.
> - **犯罪**：Courts **address** both punishment and rehabilitation.

#### ensure：确保；保证

> **核心搭配**
>
> - ensure that + 从句 确保…
> - ensure safety / quality / fairness / access 确保安全 / 质量 / 公平 / 机会
> - ensure compliance with 确保遵守
> - ensure equal opportunities 确保机会均等
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Testing helps **ensure** basic standards.
> - **科技**：Encryption **ensures** data security.
> - **环境**：Monitoring **ensures** pollution limits are met.
> - **社会/工作**：Contracts **ensure** fair pay.
> - **政府/钱**：Audits **ensure** public money is spent properly.
> - **犯罪**：Background checks **ensure** vulnerable groups are protected.

#### invest：投资；投入（时间、精力）

> **核心搭配**
>
> - invest in education / infrastructure / technology 投资教育 / 基建 / 科技
> - invest time / effort / money 投入时间 / 精力 / 金钱
> - public / private investment 公共 / 私人投资
> - return on investment 投资回报
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Nations should **invest in** early childhood education.
> - **科技**：Firms **invest in** research and development.
> - **环境**：Governments **invest in** renewable energy.
> - **社会/工作**：Workers **invest in** upskilling.
> - **政府/钱**：States **invest in** hospitals and roads.
> - **犯罪**：Programmes that **invest in** ex-offenders reduce reoffending.

#### regulate：监管；规范

> **核心搭配**
>
> - regulate the industry / market / internet 监管行业 / 市场 / 互联网
> - strictly / tightly regulate 严格监管
> - government regulation 政府监管
> - regulate emissions / advertising 规范排放 / 广告
>
> **六大话题例句**
>
> - **教育**：Authorities **regulate** private schools' standards.
> - **科技**：Laws **regulate** the use of facial recognition.
> - **环境**：States **regulate** industrial waste discharge.
> - **社会/工作**：Rules **regulate** working hours.
> - **政府/钱**：Central banks **regulate** the financial system.
> - **犯罪**：Gun laws **regulate** firearm ownership.

#### undermine：削弱；破坏（基础、信心、权威）

> **核心搭配**
>
> - undermine confidence / trust / authority 削弱信心 / 信任 / 权威
> - undermine efforts to 破坏… 的努力
> - undermine public health / safety 损害公共健康 / 安全
> - seriously undermine 严重削弱
>
